Bodo Solar Calendar Structure
Traditional Bodo months (दान) synchronized with Assam solar transitions (Sankranti / Daosri).
1. Bwisag (बैसाग / बैसागु): Mid-Apr to Mid-May • New Year / Bihu
2. Jeth (जेथो / जेथ): Mid-May to Mid-Jun • Summer
3. Aasar (आसार / आहार): Mid-Jun to Mid-Jul • Monsoon
4. Sawan (सावन / साओन): Mid-Jul to Mid-Aug • Monsoon
5. Bhadra (भाद्र / भादो): Mid-Aug to Mid-Sep • Late Monsoon
6. Aasin (आसिन / आहिन): Mid-Sep to Mid-Oct • Autumn (Current)
7. Kati (खाथि / काति): Mid-Oct to Mid-Nov • Kati Gasa
8. Aghon (आघन / आगोन): Mid-Nov to Mid-Dec • Harvest
9. Push (पुष / पूह): Mid-Dec to Mid-Jan • Winter
10. Magh (माघ / माघ्व): Mid-Jan to Mid-Feb • Magh Domasi
11. Fagun (फागुन): Mid-Feb to Mid-Mar • Spring transition
12. Chaitra (चैत्र / सत): Mid-Mar to Mid-Apr • Year-end cleansing
